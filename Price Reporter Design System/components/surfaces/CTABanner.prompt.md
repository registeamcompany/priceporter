The site's closing block: navy field, centered white heading, one bold line of copy, then the CTA (or an inline form via `children`).

```jsx
<CTABanner title="Contact Us"
  description="Contact us at 201.567.6646 or provide your details below for one hour free consultation"
  actions={<Button variant="outline-accent-dark" size="lg">Get free consultation</Button>} />
```

Tones: `navy` (default), `accent` (solid orange), `soft` (grey band). Always centered — the live site never left-aligns this block.

The standard action control — navy primary, orange accent for the single highest-intent CTA on a page.

```jsx
<Button variant="accent" size="lg" iconRight="arrow-right">Get a free consultation</Button>
<Button variant="secondary">See our services</Button>
<Button variant="secondary" onDark>Talk to an expert</Button>
```

Variants: `primary` (navy), `accent` (orange — never two side by side), `secondary` (outline), `ghost`, `link`. Sizes `sm | md | lg`. Pass `href` for links, `onDark` inside navy bands.

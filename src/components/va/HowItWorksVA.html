Four steps from "do we qualify?" to VA orders

  1. 1

    Product & service review

    We go through your catalog and tell you which items qualify for a VA Schedule — and which belong on GSA instead. That's your free one-hour assessment.

  2. 2

    We prepare the proposal

    Proof of origin, compliance certificates, TAA and FDA verification, commercial sales and pricing disclosures, a price list structured the way VA expects it. VA scrutinizes pricing harder than almost any federal buyer — so this is where we spend the most care.

  3. 3

    Submission & negotiation

    We submit your offer and work directly with your VA contracting officer — clarifications, pricing questions, final terms. Our preparation usually means fewer questions and a faster turnaround.

  4. 4

    Launch & first orders

    Your catalog goes live, we train your team on finding and answering VA requests, and we monitor solicitations so you can move before your competitors do.

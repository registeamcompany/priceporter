VA is getting stricter about pricing. That works in your favor.

VA's Inspector General has been reviewing Schedule proposals closely — and most of those reviewed needed corrections before VA could use them in negotiations. In 2026, VA also launched a new process for vendors to self-report pricing errors. Translation: VA wants vendors whose numbers are clean from day one.

That's a higher bar for companies that wing it, and an opening for those who walk in prepared. Clean disclosures, defensible pricing, and a contract that stays compliant after award — that's the part we handle.

There are five steps to securing your contract. Your primary responsibility is in the first step.

  1. 1

    Free evaluation

    In a single conversation with a GSA expert, we assess your company against GSA requirements and use market intelligence tools to evaluate federal demand for your offerings. You will receive a clear answer regarding your eligibility.

  2. 2

    We build your proposal

    We register your company with SAM.gov, assist in obtaining your Past Performance Assessment, and assemble the complete submission package, including catalog, financials, and marketing materials, for government review. You provide the necessary documents, and we handle the paperwork.

  3. 3

    We negotiate with GSA for you

    GSA negotiates prices, discounts, and terms before awarding a contract. Our experts manage this process to ensure you receive terms that support your business objectives.

  4. 4

    Award: your catalog goes live

    We publish your complete catalog on GSA Advantage, including descriptions, specifications, pricing, and images, enabling federal buyers to begin purchasing immediately.

  5. 5

    We stay on and grow it

    We conduct competitor research, optimize pricing, add high-demand products, ensure compliance, and manage modifications and renewals. Our ongoing support begins after the contract is awarded.

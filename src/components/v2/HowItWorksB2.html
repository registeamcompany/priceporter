Five steps to your contract. Your part is mostly step one.

  1. 1

    Free evaluation

    One conversation with a GSA expert: we check your company against GSA requirements and, using our market intelligence tools, measure real federal demand for what you sell. Straight answer either way.

  2. 2

    We build your proposal

    We register you with SAM.gov, help secure your Past Performance Assessment, and assemble the full package — catalog, financials, marketing materials — then submit it for government review. You supply documents. We do the paperwork.

  3. 3

    We negotiate with GSA for you

    GSA always negotiates before an award — prices, discounts, terms. Our experts lead that process so you end up with terms you can actually make money on, not just any terms.

  4. 4

    Award: your catalog goes live

    We publish your full catalog on GSA Advantage — descriptions, specs, pricing, images — so federal buyers can start purchasing immediately.

  5. 5

    We stay on and grow it

    Competitor research, pricing optimization, adding the products GSA buys most, compliance, modifications, renewals. The award is where our longest work begins.
